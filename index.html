Your ads × your flow

Keep the promise the ad made. Two of your Meta ads, two first screens.

Meta carries almost all of the Bazaart ads we can see: about 90 live in the US Ad Library, nearly all speaking to online sellers. Yet every install meets the same welcome, “Get your design superpowers”, and the question “What would you use Bazaart for? Business · Personal”, whichever ad brought it.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by where the install came from, so the first screen repeats what the ad said, and each ad is judged on the subscriptions it brings, not the installs.

Both ads already ask “Online seller?”, so “Business or Personal” isn’t asked again; “What do you sell?” takes its place (idea 1). The ads link to your App Store page, and some versions of Ad 1 to bazaart.com/edit-photos-app/. In Apple Ads you hold about 97% of the US searches for your own name, a strong brand defence; a few terms with little share, like “vacation rentals” (1%), bring people who aren’t looking for a photo editor. That data is account-level, so it can’t tell your two apps apart. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
